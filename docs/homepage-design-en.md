# UI Design Specification: NeuroShin AI Homepage

## 1. Overview
This document describes the user interface design specifications for the NeuroShin AI Homepage. As a platform providing cutting-edge AI solutions, it adopts a modern and professional design tailored for corporate users.

## 2. Design Goals
- **Professionalism:** Highlighting reliability and technical expertise for the global and Japanese markets.
- **User Experience (UX):** Intuitive navigation and clear information hierarchy.
- **Brand Consistency:** A unified design based on Primary Teal and Accent Orange.
- **Interactivity:** Sophisticated animations using Framer Motion to engage users.

## 3. Visual Identity (Color Palette & Typography)
- **Primary Color (Teal):** `#25A69A` (Represents trust and innovation)
- **Accent Color (Orange):** `#FF5A36` (Call-to-action color)
- **Background:** Calm light tones, with light teal cards for content.
- **Typography:** Sans-serif (Focusing on Noto Sans JP for a modern feel).

## 4. Layout & Structure

### 4.1 Navigation Bar
- **Elements:** Logo, Services, Products, Pricing, News, Contact Us (CTA button).
- **Behavior:** Sticky header with hover effects.

### 4.2 Hero Section
- **Headline:** Shaping the Future of Business with the Power of AI.
- **Visuals:** Gradient background with a large high-quality computer mockup.
- **Partners:** A marquee showcasing logos of major Japanese corporations (Docomo, Rakuten, etc.).

### 4.3 AI Services
- **Structure:** 6-column grid showcasing core technologies.
- **Design:** Colorful icons, service titles, and brief descriptions.

### 4.4 Stats (Achievements)
- **Content:** Number of projects, client satisfaction, and partner count.
- **Design:** Dark teal block with white high-contrast text.

### 4.5 Featured Products
- **Products:** ShinChat, DataShin.
- **Design:** Product previews with prominent Orange CTA buttons.

### 4.6 Advantages
- **Content:** Unique AI algorithms, top-tier security, 24/7 support, etc.

### 4.7 Pricing
- **Tiers:** Starter, Business, Enterprise.
- **Highlight:** The Business plan is visually emphasized to attract conversion.

### 4.8 Testimonials
- **Content:** Quotes from satisfied clients with 5-star ratings.

### 4.9 News
- **Content:** Latest AI research updates and company announcements.

### 4.10 Footer & Newsletter
- **Newsletter:** Registration form with synchronized branding.
- **Footer:** Quick links, social icons, and copyright information.

## 5. Interactions
- **Framer Motion:** Fade-in animations on scroll and button hover transitions.
- **Responsive:** Fully optimized for mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports.
